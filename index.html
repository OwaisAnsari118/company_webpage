ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

IP should be more than RTL.

Pineapplix focuses on the complete engineering package around an IP block — not just the synthesizable logic.

01 ————
DESIGN

Clean, synthesizable, parameterized RTL. Architecture that scales with requirements and targets ASIC and FPGA implementation.

02 ————
VERIFY

SystemVerilog, UVM, assertions, functional coverage and regression infrastructure — developed alongside the RTL, not after.

03 ————
INTEGRATE

Clear interfaces, register maps, parameters and integration documentation. Designed for the engineer connecting IP to a subsystem.

04 ————
VALIDATE

Simulation, lint, CDC/RDC checks, synthesis and FPGA reference implementations to validate real-world integration behavior.

We design IP with the integration engineer in mind — not just the RTL designer.

Predictable

Verifiable

ABOUT

Pineapplix is an engineering-first semiconductor IP company.

We are building reusable digital IP and verification infrastructure for the next generation of ASIC, FPGA and SoC systems.

Semiconductor IP development is a disciplined engineering practice. Every design decision has consequences at silicon — in area, power, timing and integration complexity. We take that seriously.

Pineapplix is early stage. We are building the engineering infrastructure before claiming a product portfolio.
